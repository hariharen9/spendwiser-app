Mathematical Modeling

Rent lands on the 1st.
Your month isn’t over on the 2nd.

Every other app takes your spend in the first week, divides by days elapsed, and multiplies by 30. When ₹35,000 in rent, maintenance and utilities hits on day 3, they predict you’ll go broke before mid-month.

Sovereignty

Your financial ledger belongs on your phone.

Modern fintech apps operate like data brokers pretending to be calculators. They scrape your email inbox, hold your banking tokens, and monetize your salary cadence.

SpendWiser is built with an uncompromising local-first ethos:

1

Zero Bank Credentials or Scraping

SpendWiser never asks for net banking logins, passwords, or aggregators. It parses standard on-device transaction alerts and quick voice/text entries you already control.

2

Biometrics & Configurable Auto-Lock

Protected with a hashed SHA-256 PIN and biometric authentication. Configurable post-unlock grace period (default 5 min) keeps the app fluid while you switch tasks.

3

100% Optional AI (Strict BYOK)

Zero AI requests are ever made by default. Adding your own Gemini, OpenRouter, or Claude key sends requests directly to that provider with your key. Clear it anytime to revert to offline.

4

Data Portability Guarantee

Export complete annual PDF dossiers, multi-sheet Excel statements, or raw JSON backups. Delete everything in one tap whenever you decide.
